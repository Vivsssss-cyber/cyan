# Startup Valley — Full System Context + Screen Flow Prompt

---

# 0. GAME CONTEXT (MANDATORY UNDERSTANDING)

Startup Valley is a **retail business growth simulation** with **investor-style (DCF-based) annual valuation**.

### Core Principle:
Monthly operating decisions → compound over time → impact:
- Demand
- Costs
- Staff pressure
- Inventory pressure
- Cash flow
- Retention

These outcomes accumulate into:
- Quarterly patterns (optimization layer)
- Annual strategic consequences (irreversible decisions)
- Final company & founder valuation

Valuation must feel **earned from operations**, not cosmetic.

---

# GAME CADENCE

- Total Duration: 5 Years
- Total Rounds: 60
- 1 Round = 1 Month
- Quarterly Review: Every 3 months
- Annual Strategy Gate: Every 12 months
- Annual Valuation: Every 12 months (DCF-based)

---

# REPORTING STRUCTURE

- Monthly → Operating Report (tactical)
- Quarterly → Strategic Review (trend + correction)
- Annual → Business Review + Strategic Decisions
- End Game → Investor-style report (VC / Valuator perspective)

---

# VALUE SYSTEM

Final Success is based on weighted outcome:

- Revenue → 30%
- Ownership → 50%
  - Company Valuation → 60%
  - Owner Valuation → 40%
- Cash Flow → 20%
- Additional:
  - Asset Value
  - Current Cash

---

# CORE SYSTEM RULES

## Product System
- Total Products: 10
- Active Products: Max 6
- Product swaps only at Annual Gate
- Unsold stock must be discarded before swap
- Each product has expiry
- No 7th product allowed

## Supplier System
- 3 Supplier tiers
- MOQ varies per supplier
- Distance cost fixed per supplier

## Customer System
Visible Segments:
- Value Seekers
- Balanced Buyers
- Premium Loyalists

Hidden Segments (unlock via research):
- Age
- Monetary Capacity
- Location
- Product Preference

## Employee System
- Auto-generated workforce
- Controlled via investment
- Impacts:
  - Retention
  - Hiring Lag
  - Satisfaction

## Finance System
- Cash
- Burn Rate
- Runway
- Rent
- Loan (capped by asset value)
- Interest
- Depreciation (fixed %)
- DCF Valuation
- Dilution allowed

## Forecast System
- Signals only (not prediction)
- Risk indicators:
  - Demand drop
  - Overstock
  - Cash shortage
